  • ZNF808/MER11: the liver-like off-target cells do not look like ZNF808 loss at subfamily level. ZNF808 is not lower there and MER11 is not higher. A per-copy check is pending.

1. Question and data

Question. Are endogenous retroviruses (ERVs) and other repeats of viral origin switched on at specific stages, or in specific cell types, as human stem cells differentiate into islet cells (SC-islets)? ERVs make up about 8% of the human genome. Most copies are silenced, but some LTRs act as stage-specific promoters and enhancers in early development. HERVH/LTR7 in pluripotent cells is the textbook case. We want to know whether the same thing happens on the way to beta cells, and in which lineages.

Data. The diff-multiome 10x Multiome time course: RNA and ATAC measured in the same nuclei. There are 15 samples over 8 stages (D0 hPSC → D45 SC-islets) from three differentiation batches (HZ015, HZ019/HZ021, XW002). Cell annotation comes from the shared single-cell-pipeline: ATAC_WNN_annotation, using only cells with include_downstream == True, 98,236 cells in 24 groups. This project reads those shared inputs through config.sh and never copies or writes into them.

2. Why TE quantification in single-nucleus multiome is tricky

Every design choice below exists to deal with one of these five problems.

ProblemWhat goes wrongHow we handle it
Multi-mapping readsYoung ERVs (HERVK HML-2, young HERVH, L1HS) have thousands of nearly identical copies. Reads land equally well on many of them, so aligners give them low MAPQ. Discarding multi-mappers removes exactly the elements of most interest.Count at the subfamily level first, where a read compatible with many copies of one subfamily is still unambiguous. IRescue resolves reads that fit several subfamilies with an EM step.
Intronic read-throughSingle-nucleus RNA is full of unspliced pre-mRNA. Most TEs sit in introns, so a transcribed host gene puts reads on its intronic ERVs. A cell type that expresses that gene then looks as if it "induces" the ERV.scTE with two indexes: exclusive (intronic TE reads kept) vs nointron (only TE reads outside genes). Calls that vanish in nointron are flagged. Locus-level host-gene checks come next.
3′ chemistry10x 3′ GEX only captures the 3′ end of polyadenylated transcripts. An ERV transcript is seen only if its polyA site is captured, and we see roughly where it ends, not where it starts.We keep strand information (forward-stranded) in IRescue. Locus-level work will look at read position relative to the LTR.
ATAC mappabilityCell Ranger fragments keep only MAPQ ≥ 30 reads. ATAC signal on young, repetitive elements is therefore under-counted and comes mostly from older, more diverged copies.Interpret ATAC on young families (LTR5_Hs, LTR7) cautiously; absence of ATAC signal is weak evidence.
Stage = cell type = batchEarly cell types exist in only one sample (hPSC only at D0, DE mostly at D4). Stage and differentiation batch coincide for most stages; only D7 and D9 have two batches.DESeq2 uses ~ group without a sample term, because blocking on sample would remove the contrasts we care about. P-values are therefore optimistic. Effect size and consistency across modalities matter more than padj.

4. Methods, step by step

Each step lists what it does, why we need it, how it works, and the gotchas we hit. The full script is in the collapsible block under each step, copied verbatim from the repo.

4.0 Environment and repeat annotation

What
Builds the herv_te conda env (IRescue 1.2.0, scTE, samtools, bedtools, pychromvar, scanpy). Downloads the UCSC RepeatMasker table for hg38.
Why
No TE tools existed on Alpine. RepeatMasker is the standard TE annotation and defines what a "subfamily" is (e.g. HERVH-int = internal region of HERVH, LTR7 = its LTR).
How
UCSC rmsk.txt.gz is converted to a BED with subfamily, strand, family and class (rmsk_hg38.bed.gz), plus an LTR-only subset. The env is built on acompile, which has internet access. scTE is patched because it opens files with mode 'rU', which Python 3.11 rejects.
Output
/pl/active/ZhuLab_BDC/genome/repeats_hg38/, env at /projects/hzhu10@xsede.org/software/anaconda/envs/herv_te
envs/get_repeat_annotations.sh
envs/build_herv_te_env.sh

4.1 scTE indexes and the TE BED

What
Writes the TE BED used by every counting step: 4,516,048 loci in 1,090 subfamilies, limited to the classes LTR, LINE, SINE, DNA, Retroposon and RC. It also builds the scTE genome indexes in two modes.
Why
One shared definition of "TE" keeps IRescue, scTE and ATAC comparable. Simple repeats, satellites and small-RNA genes are not TEs and would add noise. The two scTE modes are what let us separate ERV transcription from intronic read-through later (4.10).
How
scTE splits the genome into bins and records which gene or TE each bin belongs to. It assigns reads to genes first, then to TEs. In exclusive mode, a read on a TE inside a gene exon goes to the gene, but intronic and intergenic TE reads go to the TE. In nointron mode, any read inside a gene body (exon or intron) goes to the gene, so only TE reads outside genes count as TE. Gene models come from the Cell Ranger ARC GRCh38-2020-A GTF, so genes match the main analysis.
Gotcha
scTE_build -o X writes X.<mode>.idx, so symlinks hg38.<mode>.idx were added. The scTEATAC index was built but later abandoned (see 4.5).
scripts/01_build_scte_indexes.sh

4.2 Sample table and cell whitelists

What
Maps each Cell Ranger run to its analysis name (e.g. HZ180_…_hPSC_rep1 → HZ015_D0_rep1). Writes one barcode whitelist per sample with the kept, annotated cells.
Why
Counting only the 98,236 QC-passed cells makes every TE matrix line up with the annotation. It also makes the jobs much faster, because BAMs are filtered to these cells.
How
Reads the rename manifest and the annotation CSV, and keeps include_downstream == True. Barcodes are stored as in the BAM CB tag (AAAC…-1). In the merged objects a cell is named <sample>#<barcode>.
Output
configs/samples.tsv (task id = SLURM array index), $TE_OUT/whitelists/<sample>.txt
scripts/02_make_sample_table.py

4.3 RNA: IRescue subfamily counts (main RNA quantification)

What
Builds a per-cell, per-subfamily UMI count matrix from each GEX BAM.
Why
IRescue is built for 10x data and TEs. It works with UMIs (deduplicated molecules, not reads) and keeps multi-mapping reads. Reads that fit several subfamilies are shared out statistically instead of being thrown away.
How
  1. For every read from a whitelisted cell, collect all its alignments (Cell Ranger keeps secondary alignments of multi-mappers). Record the set of TE subfamilies they overlap on the correct strand.
  2. Group reads by cell barcode (CB) and corrected UMI (UB). One UMI = one molecule, so PCR duplicates collapse.
  3. A UMI compatible with one subfamily is assigned directly. A UMI compatible with several (an "equivalence class") is shared by expectation–maximisation: start from the unique counts, assign ambiguous UMIs in proportion to each subfamily's current abundance in that cell, re-estimate, and repeat until stable. --integers rounds the result.
  4. --strandedness forward: in 10x 3′ GEX, read 2 is in the sense of the mRNA, so only reads on the TE's own strand count as that TE. Antisense reads, typically from a host gene running the other way, are not counted.
Output
$TE_OUT/irescue_subfamily/<sample>/counts/ (MTX)
scripts/03_irescue_subfamily.sb

4.4 RNA: scTE counts (cross-check and read-through test)

What
Gene + TE count matrices from scTE, once with the exclusive index and once with the nointron index.
Why
It is an independent algorithm, so it checks IRescue. More importantly, the two indexes give an "intergenic only" version of every subfamily.
How
The BAM is first filtered to whitelisted cells, keeping only reads that have a corrected UMI (samtools view -D CB:whitelist -e 'exists([UB])'). scTE then assigns each read to a gene or TE through the index, counts UMIs per cell, and sums TE loci by subfamily. scTE is not strand-aware for TEs, so it also counts antisense reads. That is one reason its TE counts are higher than IRescue's.
Gotchas
scTE stops on reads without CB/UB, hence the filter. Each worker loads the whole index (~200 GB peak in total), so this runs on amem with 6 workers and 320 GB. On acpu with 60–80 GB it ran out of memory. Run time was 30–85 min per sample; job 33356420, plus 33356418_10 for sample 10.
scripts/04_scte_rna.sb

4.5 ATAC: TE-subfamily accessibility from fragments

What
For every cell, counts Tn5 cut sites falling in each TE subfamily, plus the cell's total cut sites.
Why
Chromatin opening of an LTR is the regulatory counterpart of its transcription. It is often the more direct readout of an LTR acting as an enhancer or promoter, and it does not suffer from intronic read-through.
How
Each ATAC fragment has two Tn5 insertion (cut) sites, at its start and at its end − 1. The script keeps fragments from whitelisted cells, turns each into its two cut sites, intersects them with the TE BED (bedtools intersect), and sums per cell × subfamily. A cut site inside nested TEs counts once for each TE. Cell totals are kept for normalisation. Overall, 26.9% of all cut sites fall in TEs.
Note
Cell Ranger fragment files hold only reads with MAPQ ≥ 30, so reads that map to several copies are missing here. scTEATAC (4.5b) keeps them, and 4.11 compares the two.
scripts/05_atac_te_counts.sb

4.5b ATAC, second method: scTEATAC (fixed)

What
Per-cell TE-subfamily counts from the ATAC BAM using scTE's own ATAC mode. This is an independent, published method that also keeps reads mapping to several copies.
Why it failed first
  1. scTEATAC never reads the CB tag. It runs bamToBed -bedpe | awk -F '[\t:]' '{print $1,$2,$6,$7}', so the "barcode" is the first :-separated piece of the read name. For Cell Ranger reads (LH00368:19:…, A01535:176:…) that is the sequencer ID, so each sample collapsed into 2 "cells".
  2. bamToBed -bedpe needs mates next to each other, i.e. a name-sorted BAM. The coordinate-sorted BAM produced thousands of "mate does not occur next to it… skipping" warnings and lost reads.
Fix
  1. Keep whitelisted proper pairs, dropping unmapped, secondary, supplementary and duplicate reads (-f 2 -F 0xD0C). Keep reads of every MAPQ.
  2. Put the cell barcode in front of each read name (AAAC…-1:LH00368:…).
  3. Name-sort, then run scTEATAC with -CB True.
Tested first on a small region: the left mate always came first and no pairs crossed chromosomes. Result: every whitelisted cell is recovered in all 15 samples and there are 0 mate warnings.
How it counts
Each fragment, from mate 1 start to mate 2 end, adds 1 to every TE copy it overlaps. That is the whole fragment span, not just the Tn5 cut sites. Reads that map to several copies sit on the one copy the aligner picked.
Cost
20–50 min and ~175 GB per sample on amem; each scTE worker loads the whole index.
scripts/05b_scte_atac.sb

4.6 Merge IRescue matrices and first look

What
Combines the 15 IRescue matrices into one AnnData (cells × subfamilies). Adds stage, annotation and per-cell gene UMIs, then writes QC tables and figures.
Why
To check that the counts behave sensibly (the HERVH drop after pluripotency, class composition) before running statistics.
How
Feature columns are aligned across samples. Gene UMIs come from the shared integrated RNA object (total_counts). Expression is shown as TE UMIs per million gene UMIs. This is a normalisation, not a "fraction of the transcriptome", because TE reads and gene reads overlap.
Output
irescue_subfamily_merged.h5ad, $TE_OUT/qc/irescue_*
scripts/06_merge_irescue.py

4.7 Pseudobulk (sample × cell group)

What
Sums counts over all cells of one cell group in one sample, giving one "unit". Units with fewer than 20 cells are dropped, leaving 119 units for each modality.
Why
Single-cell TE counts are very sparse. Testing on single cells treats cells as independent replicates and inflates significance. Pseudobulks summed per sample behave like bulk RNA-seq, which DESeq2 models well.
How
A sparse indicator matrix (units × cells) is multiplied by the count matrix. The unit library size is the sum of gene UMIs (RNA) or of all cut sites (ATAC). It also merges the ATAC per-cell tables into atac_te_subfamily_merged.h5ad.
scripts/07_pseudobulk_te.py

4.8 Differential testing with DESeq2

What
For each modality, two marker-style tests: each cell group vs the mean of all other groups, and each stage vs the mean of all other stages. For the stage test, units are summed per sample. A call means padj < 0.05 and log2FC > 1. Subfamilies with a mean count below 10 are dropped.
Why DESeq2
It models counts as negative binomial and shares dispersion information across subfamilies, which stabilises estimates with few replicates. It also shrinks noisy fold changes toward zero.
How
The model is ~ 0 + group, so each group gets its own coefficient (its mean log expression). The "group g vs rest" contrast uses weight +1 for g and −1/(k−1) for each of the other k−1 groups. This tests g against the average of the others on the log scale, a balanced comparison that is not driven by one big group.
Size factors are fixed to each unit's library size (gene UMIs or total cut sites), scaled to a geometric mean of 1. DESeq2's usual median-of-ratios on the TE counts would be dominated by old intronic SINEs and LINEs, which change with nuclear RNA content rather than with sequencing depth.
No sample term. hPSC exists only at D0 and DE mostly at D4, so a sample term would absorb those group effects (see §2).
scripts/08_deseq2_te.R
scripts/09_run_deseq2_te.sh

4.9 scTE merge and pseudobulk (same units as IRescue)

What
Merges scTE outputs for each mode. It separates TE columns from gene columns using the TE BED's subfamily names and pseudobulks into exactly the 119 IRescue units. DESeq2 (4.8) then runs on both modes.
Why
Using identical units and normalisation means any difference between IRescue, exclusive and nointron results comes from the counting, not from the statistics.
How / checks
scTE kept every whitelisted cell except 6 in D7_rep2. TE columns hold 50–78% of all scTE counts in exclusive mode but only ~10% in nointron mode. This alone shows how much of the TE signal in nuclei comes from introns.
Gotcha
config.sh does not export its variables, so Python jobs need set -a; source config.sh; set +a.
scripts/10_merge_scte.py

4.10 Read-through check: exclusive vs nointron

What
Gives every group-vs-rest or stage-vs-rest call from IRescue or scTE-exclusive a verdict on whether it is supported by copies outside genes.
Logic
If a subfamily is "induced" only because a host gene containing some of its copies is switched on, the extra reads land in introns. They are present in exclusive mode and vanish in nointron mode. If the subfamily's own LTRs drive transcription at copies outside genes, the induction persists in nointron.
Verdicts
intergenic also up in nointron (padj < 0.05, log2FC > 1) · weak nointron log2FC > 0.5 but not called · no_intergenic too few reads outside genes to test · intronic_only nointron log2FC ≤ 0.5, a read-through suspect
Extra metric
frac_ratio = (nointron ÷ exclusive in the called group) ÷ (same ratio in all other groups). Below 1, the group's extra TE reads come mostly from copies inside genes. Above 1, they come mostly from copies outside genes.
scripts/11_compare_scte_modes.py

Why
To see whether the missing low-MAPQ reads cost us ERV calls (young, repetitive families), and whether counting fragment spans rather than cut sites changes anything.

4.12 Candidate tiers (all methods on equal footing)

Why
Choosing either RNA method as the "main" one and applying a hard cutoff loses candidates. Of the group calls supported by copies outside genes, 27 are lost with IRescue as main and 43 with scTE as main. Most of these are threshold effects, where the other method has log2FC 0.5–1. A few are real disagreements, e.g. MER110-int in ENP, which IRescue misses.
Rule
candidate up in IRescue or scTE-exclusive (padj < 0.05, log2FC > 1) → robust the other method agrees in direction (log2FC > 0.5) and nointron is not "intronic only" → otherwise discordant, with a reason: read_through, scte_only (IRescue counts only reads on the TE's own strand, scTE ignores strand, so possibly antisense), irescue_only (scTE drops reads in gene exons), or absent_in_irescue.
ATAC support: the subfamily itself, or one of its LTRs, is up in the same group by either ATAC method.
LTR pairing
The LTRs of each internal region are taken from the genome: RepeatMasker LTR-class elements within 50 bp of the -int fragments. A subfamily is listed if it makes up ≥ 15% of these flanks (≥ 20 flanks). This recovers the expected pairs: HERVH → LTR7, HERVS71 → LTR6B/6A, HERV15 → LTR15, HERVK → LTR5_Hs/5A/5B, HERVK11 → MER11A/B/C, HERV9 → LTR12/12F, HERVIP10B3 → LTR10B1/B2.
scripts/14_candidate_tiers.py

4.13 Locus level (running)

What
IRescue -l, sense strand only, on every RepeatMasker LTR-class copy (725,763). Each copy has a unique ID <subfamily>|chrom_start_end_strand. Then:
  1. pseudobulk into the 119 units;
  2. annotate each copy as intergenic, intronic or exonic, with its host gene and whether it lies sense or antisense to it (Cell Ranger GTF);
  3. for each candidate, rank copies by their share of the increase and report n50, the number of copies giving 50% of it.
Why
A subfamily call can come from one active copy. Naming the copies shows whether an induction is a single-locus event, many copies, or a host gene. This also keeps solo LTRs (LTR12_, MLT1F2), which the Telescope proviral annotation lacks.
Checks
On sample 10, per-copy totals summed by subfamily match the subfamily run (r = 0.998), about 10% higher because LINE/SINE copies no longer compete for reads. That run took 3 min and 29 GB.
scripts/15_irescue_locus.sb
scripts/16_locus_candidates.py

4.14 ZNF808 / MER11 check

Why
De Franco et al. 2023: loss of the primate-specific KZFP ZNF808 causes pancreatic agenesis. ZNF808 represses MER11A/B/C, and in knockout differentiations MER11 copies gain activity (some at the pancreatic progenitor stage) while progenitors switch on liver genes. Our protocol makes off-target liver-like cells. Do they look like partial ZNF808 failure?
How
ZNF808, TRIM28, SETDB1 and marker genes are read from the shared counts layer, column-wise without loading the 10 GB matrix, and summed into the 119 units. MER11A/B/C and HERVK11-int signal comes from all five TE count tables on the same units. Groups are compared, especially liver vs PP1/PP2/FG.
scripts/17_znf808_mer11.py

5.5 The read-through filter changes the picture (today's result)

Only about 21% of LTR-class TE reads come from copies outside genes (nointron ÷ exclusive). For SINEs and DNA transposons it is about 10%. Fold changes agree well between IRescue and scTE-exclusive (r = 0.82), so the two counting methods agree. They agree much less between exclusive and nointron (r = 0.45), which is the read-through effect. Of 242 LTR calls from IRescue or exclusive: 129 intergenic, 45 weak, 29 too few reads outside genes to test, 39 intronic only.

Robust: supported by copies outside genes

5.7 ATAC: cut sites and scTEATAC agree

  • Fold changes: r = 0.95 across groups and r = 0.96 across stages. Per-subfamily totals correlate at r = 0.99 (log scale).
  • Calls: 462 by cut sites, 383 by scTEATAC, 337 by both. Almost every call made by one method only is a near-miss: log2FC 0.8–1.0 and significant in the other method. The rest come from the 52-cell ductal group (22 LTR calls by cut sites only).
  • Low-MAPQ reads raise counts only for young non-ERV families: L1HS 2.5×, SVA_E/F 2.2–2.4×, MADE1 3.3×, Alu 2.1×. ERV families show no gain (LTR5_Hs 0.91, HERVK-int 0.81, LTR7 0.89, HERVH-int 0.78; ratios below 1 reflect one count per fragment vs two cut sites).
  • Conclusion: cut sites stay the main ATAC measure, as the more precise accessibility readout. scTEATAC is the confirmation. ATAC support in the tiers counts either method.

5.9 ZNF808 / MER11: no sign of ZNF808 failure in the liver-like cells (subfamily level)

  • ZNF808 is not lower in the liver-like cells. It is 1.4–1.6× higher than in PP1/PP2/FG (21 vs 13–15 per million gene UMIs). TRIM28 is similar. ZNF808 is lowest in late endocrine groups (8–9).
  • MER11 is not specifically active in liver. MER11A/B/C RNA and ATAC are highest in hPSC and early endoderm and fall afterwards. Liver vs PP ratios are 0.7–1.6 with no consistent direction. MER11C is less open in liver (0.73–0.78×).
  • No anti-correlation between ZNF808 and MER11 across groups (Spearman ρ from −0.32 to +0.33 over 20 comparisons).
  • Caveat: the knockout phenotype involved a few hundred specific MER11 copies. An average over thousands of MER11 copies could hide them. To check per copy: the MER11 copies that gain H3K27ac late in the ZNF808 knockout (their clusters 5–6), in our liver vs PP cells.

6. Caveats to keep in mind

  • Optimistic p-values. Without a sample term, units from the same sample are not independent, and stage, batch and cell type are confounded. Rank by effect size and by agreement between RNA, nointron and ATAC rather than by padj.
  • Tiny groups (late_SC_delta 42 cells in units, ductal 52, exocrine 121, early_SC_delta 223, stroma 223) give unstable calls. ductal produces 37 ATAC calls from 2 units. These groups should be flagged or excluded.
  • Nointron is strict. A real ERV promoter inside an intron, for example an LTR that drives an alternative first exon of its host gene, is also dropped in nointron. "intronic_only" means "cannot tell at subfamily level", not "proven artefact".
  • scTE is not strand-aware for TEs but IRescue is. That explains part of the higher exclusive counts (e.g. MER110-int 5.6× IRescue) and the "scTE only" calls. Antisense is a hypothesis until the per-copy strand check.
  • ATAC normalisation uses total cut sites only. Groups with different signal-to-noise (TSS enrichment) shift all TE subfamilies together; liver, OTX2_endo, stroma and ductal have many calls. A GC- and accessibility-matched background (chromVAR-style) would be more robust.
  • Novelty rests on a keyword literature search. Before writing, check the pancreas / islet single-cell TE literature more carefully, including preprints.
  • Subfamily ≠ locus. A subfamily call can be driven by a single active copy. Biological claims need the locus level.
  • The README still describes the original plan (sample blocking, chromVAR). The analysis as run uses ~ group and cut-site counts. The README should be updated.

  • acompile needs QoS compile (for env builds with internet access).
